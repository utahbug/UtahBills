## Proposed daily workflow (not enabled)

1. Owner approves each source's permitted use and access method. Record scope, terms URL/version/date, any written permission, eligible content types and restrictions. Start with sources having clear permission, not all six by default.
2. Independent news job, separate schedule, permissions, artifacts and health report from official-source acquisition. Fetch allowlisted public feed URLs only; honor robots/terms, redirects, 429 Retry-After, ETag/Last-Modified, 10–15 second timeout, maximum bytes/items and backoff. No full-article scraping, paywall bypass or publisher JS execution. Suggested daily cadence is a proposal, not an enabled automation.
3. Parse with a safe XML parser: no external entities/DTD/network expansion. Project to source, GUID, original URL, canonical URL, original publication timestamp/date precision, updated timestamp, fetchedAt, headline, author and rights disposition. Validate HTTPS/host redirects, length and date. Never render publisher HTML. Unknown timezone remains explicitly unknown; do not silently assign Mountain time. Future dates and implausible timestamps go to review.
4. Triage Utah legislative relevance from permitted metadata and explicitly allowed material. Record mention text and offsets, reported title, reported sponsor and bill-number mention separately. The prototype's `reportedMentions` is deterministic triage only; literal title/sponsor hits and bill numbers without a confirmed session never generate associations. Broad terms such as MIDA create candidates only. No automated AI descriptions in this scope.
5. Editorial queue compares candidates against official session-qualified records, exact request IDs, versioned drafts and committee materials. Preserve conflicts, missing IDs, session ambiguity, historical titles, multiple proposals and news corrections. A statement about a sponsor is not evidence of 2027 membership. Require independent review before approving an association; evidence must actually connect the article's proposal to the exact official identity.
6. Publish an independently validated news snapshot atomically. On failed/empty/malformed fetch, keep that source's last accepted snapshot, mark its last successful check/staleness and surface the error in its own report. One feed failure must not block other sources or official-data publication. Never run news networking during the official build. A missing optional snapshot disables news only. Tests use fixtures, never live outlets.

## Duplication, corrections and retention

Within an outlet, source GUID and verified canonical URL identify a story; strip only known tracking parameters/fragments, preserving meaningful query parameters and path case. Prototype deduplicates source + normalized URL, rejects duplicate IDs, sorts by original publication date then stable ID. No title-only deduplication. Production needs reviewed redirect/GUID reconciliation and revision history: corrections update the same story and invalidate affected associations for re-review. Syndicated copies across outlets may be grouped only with provenance, retaining separate bylines/URLs; similar headlines about different proposals remain separate. Do not infer story equivalence from topic. Proposed 30-day rolling list and bounded archive retention require owner choice and publisher permission; prototype is a frozen sample, not a complete archive.

## Related News design and verification gate

Place a small “Related News” section after official record information on a bill/request detail, with the same date/outlet/headline/link rows and a persistent “External reporting” label. Omit section if no approved associations; show no candidate badges or speculative links. Do not overwrite official descriptions or statuses with news claims.

An association record must include article ID/canonical URL/revision hash; exact `sessionID` plus request tracking ID or bill number; relation type (direct bill link, explicit official identifier cross-reference, or independently reviewed evidence chain); official URLs, document/version IDs, retrieval dates and SHA-256 pins; supporting passage/location and rationale; reviewer identity/date; status (candidate, verified, rejected, needs-recheck); and supersession/correction history. Verify article-to-proposal and proposal-to-record links separately. An official draft link supports that draft's identity, not a request ID unless an authoritative cross-reference connects them. If no exact record can be proved, retain the article in the general listing only. No manual compound title/sponsor rule is approved by this task. An empty approved-association set is correct for all five initial proposals.
