# Phase 5E — Bill detail consolidation and visual refinement

October 25, 2026 (Mountain). Baseline: clean synchronized Main `e3b5d20c67215afb5ecc333de592c960dc9a1bc9`, successful [Phase 5D deployment 40251957035](https://github.com/utahbug/UtahBills/actions/runs/40251957035).

## Changes and evidence boundaries

- The four enhanced bills have one **Bill Summary** containing the unchanged explanation, unchanged additional qualification and every official highlighted provision in original order, including H.B. 11's nested conditions. Only the redundant presentation prefix “This bill:” is omitted; saved metadata is untouched. The list has an accessible official-provisions label. Compact metadata identifies the AI-assisted explanation's exact version, official origin of the bullets and pending independent human review.
- **Read full formatted bill** follows that summary in metadata-sized text with a 44px target. The reader remains in supporting records. Its initial selection is the explanation pin; subsequent user selections survive closing/reopening. The explicitly labeled explained-version link in Documents & sources selects the pin again. No raw XML becomes the default reading destination.
- Phase 5D added a margin to every disclosure child on top of the existing shared disclosure inset, while the reader select's ID rule reset its margin. This caused the label/field mismatch. The extra child-margin rules are removed; one shared disclosure gutter now aligns headings, labels, selectors and expanded content. A reader-field wrapper groups its label and native select; no negative margins are used.
- The existing native-select caption helper is reused for bill-detail version controls. Native selects remain interactive and accessible; their aria-hidden visible captions wrap rather than truncate. The desktop comparison control group is capped at **36rem / 576px**, 64% of the unchanged 900px legal-text area. The reader control is capped at 22rem / 352px. Narrow screens use available width; large text can increase control height above 44px. Changes to versions/sections update captions, including programmatic explained-version selection.
- The bill-detail CSS block is consolidated, replacing the Phase 5D spacing overrides. Major section gaps are 14px, supporting/roll-call/source disclosures share compact regular-weight 44px labels, and repeated disclosure borders/gaps are removed. Distinct vote records retain their own separation and directly available Official vote record links. No tallies, identities, motions or coverage qualifications are changed.
- Enhanced supporting records now have five primary disclosures: complete actions, committee details, published-version comparison, formatted reader, and **Documents & sources**. The last combines readable version PDFs, amendments/fiscal/supporting documents, exact version associations, technical XML/JSON, retrieval provenance, explanation pin and review status. PDF/XML counterparts for a supporting document share one readable destination; every distinct source URL and associated version/date remains available. Basic-record version comparisons and evidence remain available with shared spacing, control and event-date treatments.
- Parchment applies only to tagged historical legislative event/document dates in the 2026 session (including pre-session document dates), and the existing historical session marker. It does not tint 2027-session dates, future statutory effective provisions, retrieval timestamps or current contacts. Original date strings remain in complete histories and document metadata.
- **Filed at session end** is limited to the verified filed-among-bills-not-passed condition on March 6, 2026, the end date identified by the [official 2026 General Session page](https://le.utah.gov/~2026/2026.htm) and [significant session dates](https://le.utah.gov/sessionSchedule/sessdates.jsp?seltype=2&selyear=2026). Other dates use “Filed among bills not passed,” without inferring a future session endpoint. Complete history retains `House/ filed` and its original owner/date. The redundant status paragraph is removed; the pinned explanation and comparison's not-current-law qualification remain.
