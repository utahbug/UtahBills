## Official evidence investigated

- [Legislature finder](https://le.utah.gov/GIS/findDistrict.jsp) accepts street/ZIP and map selection; directs uncertain cases to county clerks. Its [loaded script](https://le.utah.gov/GIS/gis2023.js?r=6) uses 2022–2032 House/Senate layers, a geocoding GET URL containing street/ZIP, local lookup-data storage and district cookies. Embedded credentials were not copied or reused. Its current-member lookup is not a documented session-qualified roster interface; neither current contact results nor a 2026 roster establish 2027 membership.
- UGRC [House](https://gis.utah.gov/products/sgid/political/state-house-districts/) and [Senate](https://gis.utah.gov/products/sgid/political/state-senate-districts/) products identify UGRC and the Lieutenant Governor's Office as stewards. Linked feature-layer metadata describes election use from January 2022 and representation beginning January 2023, superseding 2012–2021 districts, with February 2022 geometry cleanup. House derives from enrolled HB2005 and Senate SB2006. Service names are UtahHouseDistricts2022to2032 and UtahSenateDistricts2022to2032, field DIST. Both publicly expose Query with CORS; service edit timestamps do not establish new legal effective dates. These are state legislative boundaries, not congressional districts.
- [UGRC API setup](https://api.mapserv.utah.gov/getting-started/) supports origin-bound browser keys, so static hosting alone is not the blocker. A properly managed application key would be needed; no account or key was created. [Geocoding documentation](https://api.mapserv.utah.gov/docs/v1/endpoints/geocoding/) specifies GET path parameters. It can interpolate road locations and choose tied candidates; a match score does not prove boundary accuracy or residential existence. [Privacy policy](https://api.mapserv.utah.gov/privacy/) permits temporary retention of submitted parameters for quality control and performance tuning. This conflicts with the requested address URL/log protection; retention duration and logging guarantees were not established.
- [UGRC Masquerade](https://gis.utah.gov/products/sgid/address/masquerade/) offers a public Esri-compatible interface, including POST support. However its [upstream adapter](https://github.com/agrc/masquerade/blob/4dccb4e163a6472c2da8a2e8dde1cbde355ffb78/src/masquerade/providers/web_api.py) sends the street/zone onward in a GET URL. POST at the browser boundary alone therefore does not meet the requirement. No submitted residential addresses or fabricated production results were used.

## Requirements before direct lookup can complete LEG-001

Establish a supported end-to-end interface keeping addresses out of request URLs and logs, documented retention/deletion controls, permitted use and managed credentials where necessary. Then validate authoritative public test locations, ambiguous/invalid/out-of-state cases, unavailable services and near-boundary locations; require candidate confirmation and conservative unresolved outcomes rather than arbitrary ties or unsupported distance tolerances. Preserve boundary versions/effective dates and verify exact House/Senate joins. Independently match dated official membership, profiles and sponsored legislation; keep unverified 2027 membership unavailable. Keep entered values transient, allow correction/reset, and verify privacy, keyboard/touch and assistive-technology behavior. A new server or unreliable custom geocoder was not introduced to bypass these prerequisites.
