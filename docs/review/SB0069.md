## Existing explanation — verbatim

> The second substitute expands the default student device-use restriction from classroom hours to the full school day. It retains emergency, disability-accommodation, and medical exceptions and allows additional exceptions in local policies.

> The second substitute adds a written parental opt-out for use outside instructional time, subject to limits. Read the enrolled version for the final enacted text.

<summary>Proposed wording</summary>

```text
(1) As used in this section:(a) (i) "Cellphone" means a handheld, portable electronic device that is designed to be operated using one or both hands and is capable of transmitting and receiving voice, data, or text communication by means of:(A) a cellular network;
(B) a satellite network; or
(C) any other wireless technology.
(ii) "Cellphone" includes:(A) a smartphone;
(B) a feature phone;
(C) a mobile phone;
(D) a satellite phone; or
(E) a personal digital assistant that incorporates capabilities similar to a smartphone, feature phone, mobile phone, or satellite phone.
(b) (i) "Emerging technology" means any other device that has or will be able to act in place of or as an extension of an individual's cellphone.
(ii) "Emerging technology" does not include school provided or required devices.
(c) "School hours" means the time from the beginning of the school day, as designated by the school's bell schedule, until the end of the school day, as designated by the school's bell schedule, including all instructional time, lunch periods, recess, and transition times between classes.
(d) "Smart watch" means a wearable computing device that closely resembles a wristwatch or other time-keeping device with the capacity to act in place of or as an extension of an individual's cellphone.
(e) "Smart watch" does not include a wearable device that can only:(i) tell time;
(ii) monitor an individual's health informatics;
(iii) receive and display notifications or information without the capability to respond; or
(iv) track the individual's physical location.
(2) Except as provided in Subsection (3), a student may not use a cellphone, smart watch, or emerging technology at a school during school hours.
(3) (a) Notwithstanding this section, an LEA:(i) shall establish a policy that allows a student to use a cellphone, smart watch, or emerging technology:(A) to respond to an imminent threat to the health or safety of an individual;
(B) to respond to a school-wide emergency;
(C) to use the SafeUT Crisis Line described in Section 53H-4-210;
(D) for a student's IEP or Section 504 accommodation plan; or
(E) to address a medical necessity; and
(ii) in addition to the exceptions described in Subsection (3)(a)(i), may establish a policy that provides for other circumstances when a student may use a cellphone, smart watch, or emerging technology during the school day.
(b) An LEA may establish policies that:(i) extend restrictions on student use of cellphones, smart watches, or emerging technologies to non-school hours, including
(ii) impose additional limitations on the use of cellphones, smart watches, or emerging technologies beyond those required by this section.
(4) (a) Notwithstanding Subsections (2) and (3), a parent or legal guardian of a student may submit a written opt-out request to the LEA.
(b) Upon receipt of a written opt-out request under Subsection (4)(a), an LEA shall permit the student to possess and use the student's cellphone, smart watch, or emerging technology:(i) outside of instructional time, including:(A) passing periods;
(B) lunch periods;
(C) release time; and
(D) before and after the school day; and
(ii) in a manner that does not materially disrupt instruction or LEA operations.
(c) An LEA may not require a student to have an individualized education program, Section 504 accommodation plan, medical accommodation, or special education designation as a condition for granting a parental opt-out under this Subsection (4).
(d) An LEA may continue to prohibit a student's use of a cellphone, smart watch, or emerging technology during instructional time, except as otherwise permitted by a teacher for instructional purposes.
(e) An LEA may adopt rules limiting use under this Subsection (4) to designated areas of LEA property.
(5) The state board may create one or more model policies regarding when a student may use a student's cellphone, smart watch, or emerging technology in a school during school hours consistent with this section.
```
